# Research

This directory contains source-backed research notes for MotoDarwin18.

## Principles

- Prefer primary sources and preserve source URLs.
- Separate device-specific facts from SoC capabilities.
- Mark uncertainty explicitly.
- Record source dates and exact model/revision scope.
- Do not claim compatibility from architecture similarity alone.

## Areas

- `soc/` — SoC and platform documentation.
- Additional research areas may be added as evidence and project needs become clearer.

Research notes do not establish that a working Darwin/XNU port exists.
