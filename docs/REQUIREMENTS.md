# MotoDarwin18 Requirements

This document lists the project's stated baseline requirements. It does not imply that the device is currently supported or that a working Darwin/XNU port exists.

## Required

- **Host operating system:** Windows 10 or Windows 11.
- **Linux environment:** WSL2, managed from Windows PowerShell.
- **Memory:** At least 6 GB RAM.
- **Target device:** Motorola Moto G60, exact model **XT2135-2**.

These are the complete baseline requirements currently specified by the project. No additional CPU, storage, GPU, or software-version minimums are claimed here.

## Safety and scope

- Use a device you own or have explicit permission to research.
- Keep important data backed up before any device diagnostics.
- This project is documentation and compatibility research; meeting these requirements does not mean device modification is safe, supported, or necessary.
- Do not treat requirements as instructions to unlock a bootloader, flash firmware, erase partitions, or bypass device protections.
- Verify the exact model from reliable device documentation or the device's normal settings before treating it as the target.

## Notes

Requirements may change as research progresses. Any future change should be documented with a reason and supporting evidence.
